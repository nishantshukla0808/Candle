01 · Design system
Quiet money, warm gold

What is a stock?

A stock is a small slice of a company. When you buy one, you own a tiny part of that business.

If the company grows, your slice can become worth more. If it shrinks, your slice can lose value. That is the whole game.

Quick check

You buy one share for ₹100 and sell it later for ₹120. What is your profit?

Correct. Profit = sell price − buy price = 120 − 100 = ₹20. Simple subtraction is the core of every trade.

05 · Mentor cast
Guides, not mascots

Six original mentors, one per desk. Professional identity marks — geometric, calm, distinct — instead of cartoon faces. Each has a voice: short sentences, zero jargon without explanation.
